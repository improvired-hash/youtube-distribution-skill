# Real Indian brand cases

Public YouTube data pulled with `scripts/yt_snapshot.py` on **20 Nov 2026**. "x" = views ÷ the channel's honest baseline for that format. "Likely paid" = big views with under 0.2% likes, inferred from public numbers, never confirmed. Numbers change, so re-run the script before quoting them to a client.

## 1. Ad views are not an audience: CRED (Shorts)
- **Likely paid:** "forget your wallet. bring fingers." has 23.9M views at 0.00% likes. "The more you know" has 13.9M views at 0.04% likes.
- **Organic Shorts baseline:** about 2.8K views.
- **Organic winners are celebrity-led:** T. Rajendar (551K, 197x), Rakhi Sawant on loan stacking (151K, 54x), Karan's wardrobe confessions (80K, 29x).
- **Flops:** product-mood posts like "Enchanted? Enchanted." (597 views, 0.21x).
- **Lesson:** *Draw or don't post*, and *isolate paid traffic*. Without the paid filter, the channel looks like it has 10M+ hits. It doesn't.

## 2. Topic-audience fit decides Shorts: Groww
- **Shorts baseline:** 22.3K views, at 7.2 Shorts a week.
- **Winners are a money claim plus a specific number:** "This FII is buying two stocks down 70%" (122K, 5.5x) and "PPFAS is Buying This Stock After a 53% Crash!" (76K, 3.4x).
- **Flop is a theory topic:** "Google vs Meta: Who Dominates Digital Advertising?" (5.7K, 0.25x).
- **Lesson:** the same channel, the same week, the same format. Money and status travel; theory dies.

## 3. Utility plus honesty beats hype: Ditto Insurance
- **Long-form baseline:** about 2K views.
- **Winner:** "Top 10 Best Health Insurance Plans in India 2026 | HONEST Ranking & Comparison" (21.8K, 11x).
- **Shorts winners are also rankings and concrete numbers:** term-plan rankings (5x), "₹15L, ₹25L or More?" (3x).
- **Lesson:** *utility beats novelty*. The brand acts as a trusted advisor, with no clickbait, and still gets 11x. Brand integrity and views aren't a trade-off.

## 4. Build IP, not uploads: Zerodha
- **Long-form baseline:** 13.7K views, at 5.8 videos a week.
- **Winner:** "How a hobbyist freelancer became the AVP of tech at Zerodha! | The Back Office Ep. 5" (312K, 22.8x).
- **Lesson:** a named recurring series about people, not the product. The episode promises a story the viewer wants, and the brand comes along for the ride.

## 5. Borrowed cultural moments: Lenskart and ixigo
- **Lenskart:** the Spider-Man collaboration Shorts did 107K (28x) and 46K (12x) organically, against a 3.8K baseline. The launch film (2.6M, 0.11% likes) is likely paid.
- **ixigo:** the Dussehra Short (66K) and "Tag your Kanpuriya friends" (40K) did 70-117x organically, against a baseline of about 570 views.
- **ixigo's sale announcements:** "19th Birthday Travel Sale" got about 150-230 views organically. The variants that reached 5-8K views show about 0.1% likes, so they were likely boosted.
- **Lesson:** festivals, regional identity and pop-culture moments travel when they're about the viewer. Announcements about the offer don't. Run the sale; don't expect the announcement video to carry it.

## 6. The #1 founder mistake in numbers: Razorpay (Shorts)
- **Organic Shorts baseline:** 99 views, from 2.3 Shorts a week.
- **Best performers:** about 1-1.7K views ("Naak Kat Gayi", "Bombay OGs").
- **Most uploads are product and feature clips:** "RazorSense", "Ray On Whatsapp", banking-connect promos.
- **Lesson:** consistent uploading, but about the company, with no recurring IP for viewers to come back to. Volume can't fix the wrong subject.

## 7. Hinglish plus a recognisable name plus a money question: Warikoo (Shorts)
- **Shorts baseline:** 141.5K views, at 7.2 Shorts a week.
- **Every top Short is a Hinglish money question about something viewers already know:** "Sourav Joshi Ki DEFENDER Se YouTube Par Kitni Kamai Ho Sakti Hai?" (2.2M, 15.5x), "Printer Kharidein Ya Blinkit Se Print Mangayein?" (2.1M, 15.2x), "Samay Ki Ticket Sales Se Ambani Ke Paas Zameen Aa Jayegi?" (866K, 6.1x).
- **Lesson:** the Pre-Upload Gate in action. Draw (Sourav Joshi, Blinkit, Ambani) + fit (money) + a question the viewer wants answered, written the way mass India types.

## 8. Category truth from peers: insurance (Ditto vs Beshak)
- **Winners on both channels are decision tools:** Ditto's "Top 10 Best Health Insurance Plans 2026 | HONEST Ranking" (11x), Beshak's "Which are the best term insurance plans in India in 2026?" (6.2x) and "Can you trust Niva Bupa for health insurance?" (5x).
- **Flops on Beshak are emotional stories and macro statistics:** "₹1 Crore Term Insurance Wasn't Enough: His Family Paid the Price" (0.26x), a road-accident street story (0.27x), "Indians spent over ₹2,572 crore on diabetes-related hospitalisations" (0.21x).
- **Lesson:** in a high-consideration category, viewers come to decide, not to feel. When two channels show the same pattern, treat it as the category's rule.
