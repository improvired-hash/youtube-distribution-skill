# Strategy: what a founder or brand channel should be

Run the context gate in SKILL.md first. Strategy without the outcome (brand vs sales) is guesswork.

## 1. Set the scoreboard from the outcome

| Outcome | Judge YouTube by | Don't judge by |
|---|---|---|
| Brand building | Views from the right audience, watch time, subscriber growth, branded search | Leads in month one |
| Sales | Leads, demo bookings, purchases attributed to YouTube (see `attribution.md`) | Raw views alone |

Say this out loud to the user. Most founder frustration comes from a sales goal being judged on views, or the other way round.

## 2. Diagnose the #1 mistake

Check the last 20 uploads (`scripts/yt_snapshot.py`, or ask). Flag it if most videos are:
- full podcasts, testimonials, sales videos or ads;
- about the company, not about the viewer's problem;
- one-offs with no series, no recurring format, no IP;
- posted at random intervals.

## 3. Build IP, not uploads

Recommend **one recurring format** the brand can own: a series with a name, a fixed promise and a cadence. Real proof: Zerodha's *The Back Office* (stories of people inside Zerodha) hit 22.8x the channel baseline (`references/examples.md`).

The fastest content engine for a sales-led brand: **mine sales calls.** List every question prospects ask, then answer one per video. It's low production, high intent, and every video is about the viewer.

## 4. Cadence and format mix (time-poor founder)

| Format | Realistic cadence | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Long-form, produced (case study, explainer) | 1 per week | Quality over quantity: research and packaging matter more than editing |
| Long-form, sales-call answers | Up to 1 per day | Only with good editing support. Lightly produced is fine. |
| Shorts | 1 per day minimum; 4-5 per day to be remembered | Reels feeds are saturated in 2027, so one Short is forgotten. Batch-shoot to make the volume possible. Cross-post the same cut as Instagram Reels and LinkedIn video. |

## 5. Podcast: only if it passes all three

1. **A definite identity:** a clear vision for the show, not "founder chats".
2. **An open lane:** search YouTube for shows in the niche. If 3 or more active shows already run the same interview format (e.g. "conversations with CFOs"), a generic version is closed. It needs a sharper angle, or subject knowledge nobody else has.
3. **A guest pipeline:** a dedicated outreach team. A guest-dependent show without one stalls.

Fail any of these and say no podcast: recommend a series format instead. A generic podcast is the most common expensive mistake.

## 6. Language

See `references/india-playbook.md`. Decide by who you want watching, not by what the founder speaks most comfortably.
