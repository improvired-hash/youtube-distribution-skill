# Shorts and clipping

## Rules

- **Draw or don't post.** A recognisable name or a universal hook is the biggest lever. Never lead with "Ft. [guest nobody knows]". Lead with the idea, the number or the conflict.
- **Tension words don't carry a Short.** "Worst / Truth / Biggest mistake" on their own do nothing.
- **Topic-audience fit beats clip quality.** Money, status, drama and universal emotion travel. Theory and policy framing die. Real proof: Groww's "This FII is buying two stocks down 70%" did 5.5x while "Google vs Meta: who dominates digital advertising?" did 0.25x (`references/examples.md`).
- **Clip for a standalone claim** (a number, a confession, a conflict), not for the best explanation. Re-hook at second 0 with a text overlay.
- **Don't split the seed.** Cluster Shorts around the most magnetic guest or moment. Don't dump 10 near-identical clips.
- **Volume:** 1 a day is the bare minimum, and 4-5 a day to be remembered. Batch-shoot. One cut can serve Shorts, Reels and LinkedIn.

## Picking moments from a podcast or long video

1. Read the transcript, not just the highlights an editor picked.
2. Mark every **standalone claim**: a specific number, a confession, a disagreement, a prediction, a "nobody tells you this".
3. Drop moments that need the previous 2 minutes of context to make sense.
4. Rank the moments with the gate below, and cut the top ones first.

## Pre-Upload Gate (score every Short, 0-10)

| Criterion | Points | What earns them |
|---|---|---|
| **Draw** | 0-3 | Who or what is instantly recognisable. 3: a famous person, brand or cultural moment, or a situation everyone has lived ("your first salary"). 1: a niche-known name. 0: an unknown face talking about their own product |
| **Standalone claim** | 0-3 | 3: a specific number, confession or conflict that lands with no context. 0: needs setup |
| **Topic-audience fit** | 0-2 | 2: money, status, drama or emotion. 0: theory or policy |
| **Re-hook at second 0** | 0-2 | 2: text overlay plus a spoken hook in the first second. 0: starts mid-sentence or with a logo |

**7+ post · 4-6 fix the weakest criterion and re-score · below 4 re-cut or drop.**

**No-zero rule:** a 0 on any criterion must be fixed before posting, even at 7+. A Short with a great claim but no re-hook at second 0 loses viewers before the claim lands.

Output every Short as: `Score 8/10 (Draw 3, Claim 3, Fit 1, Re-hook 1): post. Fix: tighten the opening overlay to "₹4.7L in 6 months".`

## Algo-check (before blaming the algorithm)

When a founder says "the algorithm isn't pushing us": post the highest-scoring clip as a test. If it beats the channel's honest Shorts baseline by a wide margin (3x or more), distribution works and **content is the gap**. If it also flops, look at packaging and topic fit before anything else.

## Honest baselines

Strip old viral outliers and likely-paid videos before setting targets. Use the **median**, not the mean. `scripts/yt_snapshot.py` does both.
